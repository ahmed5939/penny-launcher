# nature.glb

Trees, boulders, bushes, grass and flowers from **Stylized Nature MegaKit** by
Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html), released
under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). Credit is appreciated, not required.

Downloaded from the Poly Pizza bundle
(https://poly.pizza/bundle/Stylized-Nature-MegaKit-T34GZFA0fm) and packed by
`scripts/outpost-asset-recovery/prepare_nature.mjs`: 24 models, textures capped at
512 px with cut-out colours bled outwards, trunks/rocks/grass simplified, WebP
textures and meshopt-compressed geometry.
